🔒 100% On-Device & Offline Privacy

Privacy Policy

CalcVault: Secret Calculator • Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Core Guarantee: CalcVault does not collect, transmit, share, or sell your personal data. The app operates 100% offline with zero network permissions in release builds. Everything is encrypted locally on your physical device.

1. Overview & Commitment

CalcVault: Secret Calculator & Photo Locker ("CalcVault", "we", "our") is designed from the ground up with a strict privacy-first, zero-knowledge architecture. Your photos, videos, confidential documents, and passwords never leave your phone.

2. Data Handled Exclusively On-Device

All operations are performed locally using hardware-accelerated cryptographic primitives:

  • Master & Decoy PINs: Your PINs are never stored in plaintext. They derive 256-bit encryption keys through 120,000 PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 iterations stored in your device's hardware-backed Keystore / Secure Enclave.
  • AES-256-GCM Encryption: Every file and folder is encrypted locally with an authenticated 256-bit key and fresh 96-bit random nonce.
  • In-Memory Decryption: Media files decrypt purely in temporary volatile RAM while being viewed. No unencrypted copies are ever cached on your permanent internal storage.

4. Zero Third-Party Tracking or Ads

CalcVault contains:

  • NO Advertising SDKs: Zero commercial advertising networks (no AdMob, Unity, AppLovin).
  • NO Analytics or Telemetry: No Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, or crash aggregators.
  • NO Internet Permission: The release build completely removes android.permission.INTERNET. The operating system prevents the app from opening network sockets.

5. Security & Anti-Snoop Protection

The app enforces Android's FLAG_SECURE window flag, preventing Android from taking screenshots, recording video of the screen, or saving snapshots in the recent app switcher view.

An independent Decoy PIN allows you to open a separate, isolated decoy vault containing innocuous files if forced to unlock the device under duress.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full autonomy over your files. You can delete items or folders at any time. When you uninstall CalcVault from your device, all encrypted files, keys, and settings are permanently erased by the operating system.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

CalcVault does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The application is completely offline, non-commercial, and safe for all audiences.
